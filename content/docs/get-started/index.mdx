## What is Waylo Voice?

Waylo Voice is a platform for voice agents. You set up an agent once (what it knows, how it sounds, what it can do), and Waylo runs every call: listening, thinking and speaking in real time.

Voice agents can:

- Answer calls on a phone number.
- Talk to visitors from a button on your website.
- Call your APIs and search your documents during a call.
- Leave a transcript of every call.

## How a call works

Every turn of the conversation goes through three steps:

<Cards className="sm:grid-cols-3">
  <StageCard stage="transcriber" title="Speech-to-text">Turns the caller's voice into text.</StageCard>
  <StageCard stage="model" title="Language model">Writes the reply, and can call your tools and search your documents.</StageCard>
  <StageCard stage="voice" title="Text-to-speech">Says the reply in the agent's voice.</StageCard>
</Cards>

You choose the transcriber, model and voice per agent. Use Waylo's provider accounts, or add your own provider keys.

## What you work with

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Agents** | The instructions, voice and models. Edit a draft, publish it, roll back to an earlier version. |
| **Tools** | Your APIs, called by the agent during a call. |
| **Documents** | Files the agent searches to answer questions. |
| **Channels** | How people reach an agent: a phone number, or the Web SDK on your site. |
| **Sessions** | Every call, with its transcript. |
| **Webhooks** | Call events sent to your systems. |

You set all of this up in the Waylo dashboard, from your servers with the [API](/docs/api), or from an AI agent through the [MCP server](/docs/mcp-server).
