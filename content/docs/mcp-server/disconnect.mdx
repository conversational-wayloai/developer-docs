---
title: Disconnect
description: See and remove the agents connected to your account.
icon: SignOut
---

1. In the Waylo dashboard, open your account menu and choose **Account settings**.
2. Under **Connected agents**, find the agent.
3. Click **Disconnect** on its row and confirm with **Disconnect**.

The agent loses access on its next request. To use it again, connect it again and approve it.

Each person sees and disconnects only their own agents. Removing the agent's configuration from your client doesn't disconnect it: do it here too.
